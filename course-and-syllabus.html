About the course

Art Lab: Foundations is a mixed-media fine arts course built around observation, experimentation, and a deeper understanding of materials and process. Students learn to see carefully, draw with confidence, and make choices that give their work meaning.

The semester begins with drawing, observation, color, and visual thinking. As students build confidence, the course expands into handmade paint, mixed media, layered surfaces, and selected sculptural and cementitious processes. Along the way, students learn how artists across history have used materials, from egg tempera and gold leaf to plaster and print, to shape what their work communicates. Materials are one of the ways students learn to think and work like artists, not the entire subject of the course. The course closes with a more self-directed final project, planned, tested, and refined over a full month.

Art Lab: Foundations is designed primarily for high school students and is also an excellent fit for motivated middle school students who enjoy making, experimenting, and exploring how things work. No advanced art experience is required—students build their skills as they go.
