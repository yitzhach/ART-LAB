About the course

Art Lab: Foundations is an immersive, hands-on studio course built around a simple idea: students don't just use materials—they learn to understand them. Through experimentation, artmaking, and exploration, students discover how materials are made, how they behave, where they come from, and how artists have used them to create meaning throughout history.

Throughout the fall semester, students move through a range of studio experiences—from drawing and observation to making their own paints, exploring plaster and cementitious materials, experimenting with layered surfaces, and developing a final body of work. Some classes are focused on learning new techniques, while others function as a teacher led open studio with a focussed work session, giving students time to experiment, develop ideas, and take their projects further.

Art Lab: Foundations is designed primarily for high school students and is also an excellent fit for motivated middle school students who enjoy making, experimenting, and exploring how things work. No advanced art experience is required—students build their skills as they go.
